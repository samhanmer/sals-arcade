The Mission

Here at Sal's Physical and Digital Arcade, we strive to create an environment that's warm and inviting. We aim to create a place for everyone, from students to hang out after class- to grandpa trying get get some nostalgia.

From people who like videogames, to people who enjoy table top RPGs- and even people who just want a retro environment to read a comic book- that is the place we hope to be for you.
